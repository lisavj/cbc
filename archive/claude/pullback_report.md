# CBC minimum-pullback study (10/27/2026)

Question: before a with-trend entry (long: pullback = highest high since the HTF CBC turned up − entry close; short mirrored), is there a minimum pullback that improves LTF EMA20 Pierce, HTF EMA9 Pierce and HTF EMA20 Pierce entries? Example: long, high 4100, entry 4090 → pullback 10 pts.

Data: replayed alerts from the BarFeed archive (3m 01/05→10/27, 2m 06/01→10/27; same replay as alert_param_sweep.py, 76–94 % match with stored alerts), ~800–1,900 with-trend fires per case and type. Outcomes (a) opposite HTF flip / 17:00 / 2 h, (b) 1.3 ATR stop / 2.0 ATR target. "Robust" = the blocked setups lose in all three thirds under both outcome rules. Cross-check: live CBC setups 10/19→10/27 with real results (small samples).

## Findings

**Typical pullback** (median, since flip): MGC 3m ≈ 6–7 pts (1.5–1.8 ATR), MES 3m ≈ 5 pts (1.4–1.7 ATR), 2m charts ≈ 3.5–4.7 pts. A 10-pt MGC pullback is ≈ 2.5–3.5 ATR — the deep end, which is not better.

**LTF EMA20 Pierce**
- **MGC 2m: minimum 0.75 ATR (≈ 2 pts) is the only robust minimum found.** Blocks 206 of 1,418 (15 %), blocked net (a) −305 / (b) −162, losing in all three thirds. Live setups agree: pullback < 0.75 ATR 8 setups −26.0 vs 0.75–2.5 ATR 24 setups +17.4. 1.0 ATR and up stop being robust.
- MGC 3m: same direction (0–0.5 ATR bucket weakest under rule b; live < 0.75 ATR 8 setups −19.9) but rule (a) disagrees → watch, not evidence.
- MES 3m: no minimum helps. MES 2m: the opposite — shallow pullbacks are better; *deep* ones (> 1.5 ATR) lose in all thirds under both rules (but that cuts 45 % of fires, kept avg only +0.17).

**HTF EMA9 Pierce — no minimum; shallow is best.** On all four cases pullbacks < 1 ATR have the highest win % (53–60 %) and avg, in every third; deep pullbacks (> 2–2.5 ATR) are the weakest. Not a time-since-flip effect (shallow late entries are also strong). Robust *maximum*: **MES 3m, block pullback > 2.5 ATR** (329 blocked, (a) −469 / (b) −265, all thirds) or > 3 ATR (150 blocked). MGC 3m: max 1.5–2 ATR helps under (b) in all thirds but not (a). Live 1-week setups don't confirm yet (MES 2m < 0.75 ATR 8 setups −43.6).

**HTF EMA20 Pierce — nothing.** No minimum or maximum is robust on any case.

**Verdict:** no CBC change yet. Candidate to test: `MGC 2m` LTF EMA20 Pierce minimum pullback 0.75 ATR. Watch: HTF EMA9 maximum pullback 2.5 ATR on MES 3m. Caveat: replay outcomes are not CBC's real setup exits, and ~100+ settings have been tried across the studies, so confirm on live setups (20+ per bucket) before changing.

Script: claude/pullback_study.py (needs MES.json / MGC.json / MES_2.json / MGC_2.json from /bars?sym=<KEY>&days=400&from=2026-01-05 — 2m from 2026-06-01, the server 502s on the larger 2m request).
